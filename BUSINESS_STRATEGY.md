## 🧠 PSYCHOLOGICAL ENGAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

### 1. **Hook Model (Nir Eyal)**

#### Trigger
- **External:** Push notifications with personalized messages
- **Internal:** FOMO (Fear of Missing Out), loneliness, curiosity

#### Action
- **Minimal friction:** One-tap actions (swipe, like, boost)
- **Variable rewards:** Unpredictable matches and messages

#### Variable Reward
- **Tribe:** New matches, compliments, profile views
- **Hunt:** Discovery of interesting profiles
- **Self:** Profile completion achievements

#### Investment
- **Profile building:** More effort = better matches
- **Daily engagement:** Streaks and rewards
- **Premium features:** Unlockable content

### 2. **Dopamine-Driven Mechanics**

#### Instant Gratification
- ✅ Immediate match notifications with celebratory animations
